# Profacciata website design brief

## Design read
A premium coating contractor website for homeowners, architects, builders and commercial property teams. It should feel modern, architectural and credible rather than like a generic paint catalogue.

## Concept spine
Material in Motion. The visitor begins with a building-scale view, moves closer to the surface, then explores finish families and a clear project enquiry path.

## Delivery tier
Cinema. Use the prebuilt scroll-scrub runtime for one continuous architectural opening sequence, followed by restrained editorial sections and a direct enquiry path.

## Animation mode
animated-website

## Journey shape
single-shot. A single continuous camera push-in on one facade is more coherent than stitched scenes and keeps the experience economical. A procedural 3D render is used as the source for a lightweight scroll-scrub video because the current generation account has no usable paid or unlimited allowance.

## Journey
1. Establish the building: “Surfaces built to belong.” The hero introduces the contractor and the building as a whole.
2. Approach the surface: the visitor's scroll scrubs a continuous push-in that brings the facade's coating texture closer.
3. Explore finish families: illustrative references for textured plaster, concrete-look surfaces, dimensional feature walls and exterior textures.
4. Understand the approach: assess the surface, align the finish, plan the application.
5. Review the portfolio index: show the fourteen project names supplied by the company without attaching unverified online images.
6. Start an enquiry: prepare an email using the visitor's details and explain that the site does not store or submit form data.

## World grammar
One contemporary architectural world with realistic proportions, mineral plaster, dark glazing, bronze-coloured trim and restrained landscaping. Slow movement, no cuts, no text inside the movie, and stable geometry throughout.

## Palette
- Mineral paper: #F3F3EF
- Charcoal ink: #252A27
- Deep evergreen accent: #41665A
- Soft stone: #D7D9D2
- Secondary text: #69736D
This palette is a starting point for the luxury architecture and modern professional direction. The original logo, when supplied, remains the authority for final brand-colour adjustments.

## Typography
A modern grotesk display face with an available sans-serif fallback. Use restrained uppercase labels, clear hierarchy, generous spacing and readable body copy. Do not imitate the business-card logo with generated type.

## Layout system
- Slim, single-line responsive header.
- Full-viewport scroll-scrub hero.
- Asymmetric editorial introduction.
- Material-study grid with one dominant image and smaller supporting studies.
- Numbered application approach.
- Project index with the fourteen supplied names.
- Direct enquiry section.
- Quiet footer.

## Motion and camera
One continuous, gently easing push-in from an establishing facade view toward a textured detail. The current media is an MP4 derived from a procedural 3D render with a subtle digital push-in. It is not a generated AI video and should be replaced only if a better approved asset becomes available.

## Asset plan
- Procedurally modelled 3D facade scene and a short scroll-scrub MP4 derived from its approved camera render.
- Poster extracted from the encoded MP4's exact first frame.
- Four procedural material-study images marked as illustrative, not supplier-specific product photography.
- Original Profacciata logo to be supplied and inserted unchanged.
- Social preview artwork derived from the architectural render; favicon remains a neutral placeholder until the original logo asset is provided.
- Project media remains a text index until each image is matched to the project and reuse permission is confirmed.

## CTA inventory
- Header: Get a quote.
- Hero: Explore finishes.
- Hero: View projects.
- Enquiry: Prepare email.

## Business and material accuracy
Profacciata executes coating projects. Materials are sourced from suppliers including Luxture and other manufacturers. Do not imply Profacciata manufactures all materials, has exclusive supplier partnerships, or guarantees performance without evidence. Do not show supplier logos or catalogue imagery until image-use permission is confirmed. Never present generated or procedural images as completed customer projects.

## Launch gates
1. Original company logo supplied and integrated without redesign.
2. Owner verifies phone, email and exact brand/tagline wording.
3. Project-media permission and attribution checks completed before project photos go live.
4. Supplier image and logo permissions confirmed before those assets appear.
5. Enquiry flow clearly opens the visitor's email application and does not claim to store or submit data.
6. Typecheck, build and browser checks completed before deployment.
